<h1 align="center">Background of Study</h1>
  
  KTM Berhad (KTMB) is the main rail transport operator in Peninsular Malaysia, serving thousands of passengers
every day through key services like the Electric Train Service (ETS) and KTM Intercity. As smartphones have 
become an essential tool for daily travel, the official KTMB mobile app acts as the primary gateway for usrs to
check train schedules and purchase tickets. However, despite being such a crucial public service, the current
app will struggles with basic usability issues that create unnecessary friction and frustration for 
passengers during the booking process.

  This redesign initiative is directly motivated by real-world issues that passengers experience on the current
platform. The main problem is the visual clarity during seat selection. Usually, users have to pick seats 
without knowing where is the toilet, power sockets or luggage compartments. Not only that, theres no symbol 
showing which seat is facing forward or backward. On top of that, the app lacks a direct, real-time 
customer support channel, leaving passengers stuggling with questions on the issue they encounter during 
operational hours and after operational hours.

  Therefore, to solve these current challenges, this project introduces some redesign on some parts of KTMB app
which focuses on visual transparency, automated suppport and login security. The new interface replaces the static
seat booking screen with an interactive visual cabin that shows symbol of selected things that are going to the 
redesign phase. For customer service, theres 24/7 Meta AI assistant powered by Natural Language Processing (NLP) 
which it helps to handle inquiries instantly while waiting for the staff during operational hour. Finally, 
Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) are implemented for Face ID login to ensure secure and fast access.
